# REDLINE RIDERS

A retro 3D arcade motorbike racer. Lean into the corners, fire your nitro on the straights and try not to meet the guard rail at 250 km/h.

Everything is made in code: the bikes, riders, tracks, scenery, music and sound effects. No libraries, no downloads, and it works offline.

## Play

Open **`index.html`** in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari (double-clicking the file works). A computer with WebGL is required (any machine from the last ten years).

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Throttle | `↑` / `W` | A / RT |
| Brake | `↓` / `S` | B / LT |
| Steer / lean | `←` `→` / `A` `D` | Stick / D-pad |
| Nitro | `Space` / `Shift` | X |
| Shift up / down (manual gears) | `E` / `Q` | RB / LB |
| Camera (chase / far / helmet) | `C` | Y |
| Pause | `Esc` / `P` | Start |
| Music on/off | `M` | |

Menus: arrows + `Enter` (or the mouse click / A button), `Esc` to go back.

## Modes

- **Championship** — pick one of four starter bikes, then race 8 cups (United States, South America, Japan, Germany, Scandinavia, France, Italy, United Kingdom), 4 tracks each. Finish a cup in the top 3 for a trophy and to unlock the next one. Earn prize money, buy upgrades in the **Shop** (engine, exhaust, tires, gearbox, nitro, suspension), new bikes at the **Dealer**, and repaint them in the **Garage**.
- **Quick Race** — any bike, any of the 32 tracks, 1–9 laps, 0–11 rivals.
- **Time Trial** — ride alone against the ghost of your best lap.
- **Multiplayer** — coming soon (split-screen and online).

## Tips

- Hitting the rail or a rival hard throws you off the bike. You get back on after a few seconds, but you lose a lot of time.
- Brake **before** a turn, not in it. Chevron signs mark the tight ones. Your tyres only have so much grip: braking hard while leaned over leaves less for turning, and if you keep asking for more the front tucks and you slide off (a **lowside**). The tyres squeal and "FRONT SLIDING" flashes first, so ease off the brake or stand the bike up. Easy difficulty never lowsides you.
- **Slipstream**: tuck in right behind a rival on a straight and you go faster. Stay in the tow for two seconds and you get a nitro charge back. Then pull out and pass.
- The rivals are not all the same. Viper, Raven and Max Power will move across to block you; Blaze, Sable, Ziggy and Turbo Tina brake late and sometimes overcook a corner; Jolt, Rusty and Dash are rookies. Watch for a rival running wide - that's your gap.
- Grass slows most bikes down a lot, but the supermoto doesn't mind.
- Rain and snow reduce grip.
- Nitro also pulls a little wheelie: use it on straights.

## Options

Difficulty, automatic/manual gears, km/h or mph, camera, 3D resolution (from chunky 216p up to 648p), colour dithering, the classic "vertex wobble", CRT scanlines, and music/effects volume. Progress saves automatically in the browser.
